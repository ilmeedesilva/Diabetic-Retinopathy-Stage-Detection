# RetinaBot knowledge base — sources & disclaimer

`dr_knowledge.jsonl` contains short, factual statements about diabetic retinopathy
used to ground the RetinaBot assistant. Each entry carries a `source` label
indicating the body that publishes guidance in that area:

| Label | Body |
|-------|------|
| ICO | International Council of Ophthalmology — *Guidelines for Diabetic Eye Care* |
| AAO | American Academy of Ophthalmology — *Preferred Practice Pattern: Diabetic Retinopathy* |
| NHS DESP | UK NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Programme |
| WHO/IAPB | World Health Organization / International Agency for the Prevention of Blindness |
| DCCT/UKPDS | Landmark trials on glycaemic control and retinopathy progression |

**Disclaimer.** These entries are concise educational paraphrases of
well-established, non-controversial clinical facts, compiled by the project author
for this coursework. They are **not** verbatim guideline text and must not be used
for clinical decision-making. Referral timeframes and screening intervals vary
between national programmes; always follow local protocol. The assistant appends a
"not a diagnosis / see a professional" notice to every answer.
